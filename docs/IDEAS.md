# [App Name] - Raw Ideas

_These are raw ideas that need to be processed into a proper TODO entry. Check it off here once accounted for in the TODO._

_For bug reports (things that are broken NOW), use [BUGS.md](BUGS.md) instead. IDEAS.md is for future possibilities; BUGS.md is for current problems._

_Instructions: Dump ideas here as they come up during development. Don't filter or organize, just capture. Periodically (during Phase 6: Strategic Review, or whenever the list gets long), process each idea: evaluate it, create a TODO entry if it's worth building, and check it off here with a note about where it landed._

> � **Guidance:** Format:
> - [ ] Raw idea as it occurred to you
> - [x] Processed idea → Added to TODO > [Section] > [Item name]
> - [x] Evaluated and rejected → [brief reason]
>
> 💡 **Lesson learned:** The checkbox + mapping pattern creates accountability and prevents "idea debt" (a growing list of unprocessed ideas that creates anxiety without producing action). When processing, be willing to reject ideas. Not every idea deserves a TODO entry.
>
> 💡 **Lesson learned:** Keep ideas and bugs in separate files. IDEAS.md is low-urgency (evaluated in batches during strategic review). BUGS.md is higher-urgency (triaged as discovered). Mixing them causes bugs to get buried under ideas, or ideas to feel like emergencies. If something is broken, it goes in BUGS.md. If something could be better, it goes here.
