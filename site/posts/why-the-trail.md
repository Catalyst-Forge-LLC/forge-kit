title: Why “trail,” not “kit”
date: 2026-08-19
description: The name is the product idea. The work leaves a path, a record, and lessons the next project can follow.
tags: [methodology, getting-started]
---

ForgeTrail is not a tracking or logging product. It is a **persistent development system for building software with AI agents**.

**Trail** is the right word because it names several things the system does at once:

- the **path** through the development lifecycle
- the **record** of where the project has been
- **decisions and gotchas** left behind
- **breadcrumbs** for resuming work
- **lessons** that future projects can follow
- the act of **blazing a better trail** through AI-native development

A loop describes a mechanism. A trail describes the **artifact of accumulated experience**.

`Forge` works two ways, and both are useful. As a noun: the trail associated with the forge. As a verb: forge a trail, create a new path forward, and keep the trail behind you.

The first sentence still has to name the category. Then the metaphor earns its keep.

**Forge the path. Keep the trail.**
